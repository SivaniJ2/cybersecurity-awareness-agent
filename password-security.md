# Password Security

## Why password security matters

Passwords are commonly used to protect accounts and information. Weak, reused, or easily guessed passwords can make accounts easier for attackers to compromise.

## Creating strong passwords

Use passwords or passphrases that are:

- Long and difficult for others to guess.
- Unique to the account or service.
- Not based on easily discoverable personal information.
- Easy for you to manage securely.

A password manager can help generate and store unique passwords for different accounts.

## Avoid reusing passwords

Do not use the same password across multiple important accounts. If one account is compromised, reused passwords could put other accounts at risk.

## Protect your passwords

- Never share your passwords with other people.
- Do not send passwords through email or messaging services.
- Avoid entering passwords into websites reached through suspicious links.
- Be careful when entering passwords on shared or public computers.
- Use a password manager when appropriate.

## Change passwords when necessary

A password should be changed if you believe it has been exposed or compromised. Follow the security procedures provided by the organization or service.

## Use multi-factor authentication

Where available, enable multi-factor authentication (MFA). MFA provides an additional layer of protection beyond a password.
