# Safe Browsing

## What is safe browsing?

Safe browsing means using websites and online services carefully to reduce the risk of malware, phishing, scams, and unauthorized access to accounts or information.

## Check websites before interacting

- Be cautious when visiting unfamiliar websites.
- Check the website address before entering sensitive information.
- Be careful with links received unexpectedly through email, messages, or social media.
- Do not assume a website is trustworthy just because it looks professional.
- Use bookmarks or trusted sources to access frequently used websites.

## Be careful with downloads

- Download software and files from trusted sources.
- Avoid downloading unexpected files from suspicious websites or messages.
- Be cautious with executable files and unexpected attachments.
- Keep your operating system, browser, and applications updated.

## Protect your information

- Avoid entering passwords or sensitive information on suspicious websites.
- Do not provide personal or financial information unless you understand why it is required and trust the website.
- Use secure authentication practices, including MFA where available.

## Watch for suspicious activity

Be cautious if a website or message:

- Claims that you must act immediately.
- Requests sensitive information unexpectedly.
- Displays unusual warnings or pop-ups.
- Attempts to make you download an unfamiliar file.
- Redirects you to an unexpected website.

## If something seems suspicious

Stop and verify the website or request using a trusted source. Do not continue simply because a message or website creates a sense of urgency.
